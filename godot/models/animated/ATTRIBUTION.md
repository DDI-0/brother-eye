# Animated character attribution

The character meshes and transferred motion are derivatives of these supplied works, licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/):

- [Human](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/human-03a70758739544b3aa705c13af3872b1) by **aaron.kalvin**. Enhanced clothing, materials, hair and details, normalized scale/origin, biped skeleton, skin weights and baked motion were added for Brother Eye. Original Human metadata is preserved in `docs/licenses/human.json`; Stage 3A attribution also accompanies the static derivative.
- [Space Engineers Astronaut](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/space-engineers-astronaut-02a99eb6a6214c9cabbd321c2ee6ab86) by **Keen Software House**. Supplied suit, helmet, gloves and backpack geometry retained; scale, materials, biped rig, skin weights, rigid armor bindings and animation added.
- [Walk Cycle Animation](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/walk-cycle-animation-e7bddd2088434d82893e14f2bc76a5aa) by **jlwande2**. Evaluated joint directions were transferred to both rigs, corrected for facing/side conventions, converted to in-place motion and adjusted for floor contact.
- [Standing / Sitting Animation](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/standing-sitting-animation-09b82fda6ec54d06b740dfd56660b248) by **abysmaljess**. The sit-to-stand segment was transferred and reversed for sit down, with scale/floor adjustments, reduced torso lean and a seated idle derivative.

Idle and run are original motion authored for this project. No mesh, texture, keyframe or sampled motion from the supplied **CC-BY-NC run.zip** is present in these runtime assets.

Original downloads and full supplied notices remain under `assets/source/` and `docs/licenses/`. Retain this attribution when sharing the derivatives.
